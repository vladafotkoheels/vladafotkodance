Heels Dance is where

In this course, you will learn the essential techniques of Heels Dance, including walks, body movement, turns, floorwork, and choreography. Every lesson is designed to help you build confidence in your movement and develop strong technical skills step by step.

There is something special about dancing in heels. The moment you put them on, the way you move begins to change.

The moment you put them on, the way you move begins to change. Your posture shifts, your lines become longer, and you become more aware of your body and the space around you.

Inside the course

  1. 01

    Foundations
    & Mindset

    Start your heels journey with the right foundation. In this module, you’ll learn the core principles behind heels dance, develop a stronger connection with your body, and begin understanding how movement can become a powerful form of self-expression.

  2. 02

    Walking
    Techniques

    Learn the essential walking techniques used in Heels Dance, including posture, weight transfer, balance, bevel, a classic walk, and a side walk. These fundamentals will become the foundation of your movement.

  3. 03

    Body
    Movement

    Discover how to create fluid, controlled movement through your entire body. In this module, you’ll learn body rolls, body waves, and movement techniques that will help you dance with greater freedom and body awareness.

  4. 04

    Turns &
    Pirouettes

    Build a strong technical foundation for turns and pirouettes. You’ll learn proper technique, spotting, balance, and exercises designed to improve control, stability, and coordination.

  5. 05

    Floorwork

    Learn beginner-friendly floorwork step by step. This module focuses on technique, body control, transitions, and helping you feel comfortable moving on the floor safely and gracefully.

  6. 06

    Choreography

    Learn a complete heels choreography while expanding your movement vocabulary and exploring new combinations, textures, and ways of moving. This module will help you develop greater musicality, performance quality, and freedom in your dance.
